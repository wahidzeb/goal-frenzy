How to play

Touch controls

Move with your left thumb. You always control the player with the yellow ring. Control switches to whoever is best placed. With Full assist (the default), your player keeps running and chasing on their own when you let go of the stick.

SHOOT: hold to build power, then release. Shots are aimed into the corner away from the keeper. Push the stick up or down while you shoot to pick a side. Swipe up or down on the button as you let go to curl the ball.
PASS: tap for a ground pass to the teammate marked with the white arrow. Hold for a lofted through ball.
SPRINT: hold to run faster while your stamina lasts.
POWER: once the ring is full, tap to use your power shot.
SWIPE TO SHOOT: swipe anywhere on the right half of the screen. The length sets the power, the angle picks the corner, and a curved swipe bends the ball. Swipe backwards to pass that way, or tap to pass to the best open teammate. Without the ball, swipe to slide tackle or tap to switch player. You can turn this off in Settings.

Without the ball, the buttons change: SLIDE makes a slide tackle and SWITCH changes player. Running into an opponent who has the ball can also win it from them.

Keyboard

WASD or arrows move · Space/J shoot or slide (hold for power) · X/K pass (hold to lob) · Shift sprint · E/Q power · Tab/C switch player · P/Esc pause

2 Players (same device)

Pick 2 PLAYERS on the Quick Match or Penalty Shootout screen, then choose a team and a home or away kit for each player. Hold the phone sideways. P1 plays on the left half of the screen and P2 on the right half. Each player has their own stick at the outer edge and their own buttons toward the middle, and both can touch at the same time. In a 2-player shootout you take turns: the shooter swipes up on their half, and the keeper taps the mini goal on their half to dive.
Keyboard: P1 WASD move · F shoot · G pass · Left Shift sprint · R power. P2 arrows move · K shoot · L pass · J sprint · O power (numpad 1 2 0 3 also work). In a shootout, the shooter aims with their move keys and the keeper dives with theirs. 2-player games don't award coins.

Power shots

Your power meter fills over time, and faster when you pass well, win tackles and shoot. Opponents have a power too: the coloured bar under each team's score panel shows how full it is. Buy and upgrade powers in the Shop.

Rules (arcade)

There are no fouls and no offside. The ball goes out for throw-ins, corners and goal kicks, and restarts are taken quickly. Crosses and corners set up headers and volleys. Knockout games that end level go to a penalty shootout.

Penalties

When shooting, swipe up from the ball toward the spot you want to hit. The direction picks left or right, the length picks the height (too long goes over the bar), and a curved swipe bends the ball. When you are in goal, tap where you want to dive. If you dive too early, smart penalty takers will put it the other way.

Progress

You earn coins from every match, and more for wins, goals, harder difficulties and trophies. Spend them in the Shop to upgrade your club's Pace, Shooting, Passing, Defending, Goalkeeper and Stamina, or to unlock new powers. You can rename your club and design its kit in My Club.
